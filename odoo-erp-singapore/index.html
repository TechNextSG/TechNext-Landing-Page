Common Questions

Odoo ERP Singapore — FAQ

How long does Odoo implementation take for a Singapore SME?
For a typical Singapore SME (20–100 staff), a standard Odoo implementation covering Sales, Inventory, and Accounting takes 8–12 weeks from kick-off to go-live. Larger deployments with Manufacturing or multi-company setups typically run 14–20 weeks. We provide a detailed milestone plan in week one.
Does Odoo handle Singapore GST and IRAS compliance?
Yes — Odoo 18 includes localised accounting for Singapore including GST tracking, IRAS-compatible tax reporting, and multi-currency support. TechNext configures this correctly during implementation so your finance team is compliant from day one.
Can we migrate from SAP to Odoo without losing data?
Yes. We've completed SAP-to-Odoo migrations for multiple clients with full data integrity. The process involves data mapping, transformation scripts, parallel-run testing, and a controlled cutover window — typically on a weekend to minimise disruption. All historical data is preserved.
What Odoo modules do Singapore businesses use most?
The most-deployed modules for Singapore businesses are: Accounting (GST, multi-currency), Sales & CRM, Inventory & Purchasing, HR & Payroll (CPF-compatible), and Project Management. Manufacturing and Field Service modules are common for industrial clients.
Do you offer ongoing Odoo support after go-live?
Yes — all TechNext clients get a dedicated support arrangement post-go-live. This includes bug fixes, minor customisations, Odoo version upgrades, and a monthly check-in call. We don't disappear after launch.
